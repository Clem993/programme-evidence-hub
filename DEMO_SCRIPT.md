# Eight-to-ten-minute demonstration script, v0.2.3

## 1. Establish the boundary

Open the Programme overview and point to the synthetic-data notice. State that the prototype demonstrates information structure, policy, provenance and human review, not a real programme recommendation.

## 2. Show the role policy

Remain in the Translational & Preclinical Lead role. Explain that the view uses `PEH-GAC-001` policy version 1.0.1. Role selection simulates policy behaviour; it does not authenticate the user. Switch briefly to Programme Manager and note that restricted sources become gated.

## 3. Open the Source Registry

Select `SRC-003`. Show its owner, version, authoritative location, SHA-256 checksum, ingestion lineage and terminology mappings. In the Programme Manager role, show that the metadata remains visible while the restricted artefact is gated. Switch back to Translational & Preclinical Lead and open the artefact.

## 4. Inspect a retrieval trace

Open Evidence Explorer and select `E-012`. Walk through the query, retrieval route, ontology concepts, exact source location and human review status. Emphasise that the trace is inspectable and the evidence retains its uncertainty.

## 5. Demonstrate a decision-change trigger

Open Signals & Suggestions and introduce the simulated external safety update. Show that the system links the signal to the safety-margin criterion and requests human review. Show the ticket opening date, due date, and days open. Accept, amend, or assign the suggestion and show the audit record. Accepted, rejected, and amended tickets retain their resolution time; tickets assigned for review remain open.

## 6. Show the human-owned decision

Open Decision Workspace. Edit and save the rationale, then record a governance review with a permitted role. Explain that the policy disables automated approval.

## 7. Review the golden questions

Open Evaluation. Show an omitted safety finding, an irrelevant retrieved item and an incorrect source locator. Point out that these error types are reported separately, then show the human disposition for each question.

## 8. Finish with handover

Generate the Handover Package. Show that the receiving team gets the decision, evidence, unresolved uncertainties, actions and reconsideration triggers, with role and policy context retained.
